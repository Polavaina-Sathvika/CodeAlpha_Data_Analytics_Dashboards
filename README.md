# 📊 CodeAlpha Data Analytics Dashboards

A collection of interactive **Power BI dashboards** developed as part of the **CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship**.

This project focuses on transforming datasets into meaningful visual insights using Power BI, DAX, interactive filters, and data-driven analysis.

---

## 🎯 Projects Included

### 1. 💰 Financial Health Dashboard

An interactive dashboard designed to analyze financial performance and identify important business trends.

**Key Features:**
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total COGS
- Profit Margin
- Total Units Sold
- Profit contribution by product
- Sales vs Profit analysis
- Profit drivers analysis
- Sales by country
- Profit margin target
- Sales trend and forecasting
- Interactive Country, Segment and Product filters

---

### 2. 👩‍💼 Human Resources Analytics Dashboard

An HR analytics dashboard designed to understand workforce distribution, employee attrition and employee performance.

**Key Features:**
- Total Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Active Employees
- Average Job Satisfaction
- Average Performance Rating
- Employee distribution by department
- Attrition analysis by department
- Workforce distribution by job level
- Employee distribution by age
- Department-wise HR performance summary
- Interactive Department, Job Role and Gender filters

---

### 3. 🎓 Educational Performance & Resource Allocation Dashboard

An educational analytics dashboard created to analyze student academic performance, engagement and areas requiring academic support.

**Key Features:**
- Total Students
- Average Math Score
- Average Absence Days
- Average Study Hours
- Subject-wise academic performance
- Performance comparison by gender
- Student engagement analysis
- Average absence by career aspiration
- Interactive Gender, Career Aspiration and Part-Time Job filters
